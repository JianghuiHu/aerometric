# AEROMETRIC AI entry

Create, adapt, or review a drone GLB for AEROMETRIC Studio.

Read in order:

1. `MODEL_REQUIREMENTS.md`
2. `MODEL_STRUCTURE.md`
3. `MATERIAL_GUIDE.md`
4. `GLB_EXPORT_GUIDE.md`
5. `ACCEPTANCE_CHECKLIST.md`

Use `schema/aerometric-profile.schema.json` for sidecar mappings. Do not merge controllable parts, alter real dimensions for presentation, or claim a capability that has not been verified. Export and re-import the final GLB before reporting completion.
